Various Types of Indian Food

Mughlai Food

Mughlai cuisine is a style of cooking developed in the Indian subcontinent by the imperial kitchens of the Mughal Empire. It is known for its rich, aromatic dishes that often feature a blend of spices, cream, and nuts.

Tamil Food

Tamil cuisine is the cuisine of the Tamil people of South India and Sri Lanka. It is characterized by its use of rice, lentils, and a variety of spices. Popular dishes include idli, dosa, sambar, and rasam.

Punjabi Food

Punjabi cuisine is the cuisine of the Punjab region of India. It is known for its hearty and flavorful dishes, often featuring dairy products like paneer and ghee. Popular dishes include butter chicken, chole bhature, and sarson da saag.

Northern Most Eaten Food

Northern Indian cuisine is diverse and includes a variety of dishes from different states. It is known for its use of wheat, dairy products, and spices. Popular dishes include roti, paratha, dal makhani, and various kebabs.

Himalyan/Mountain Food

Himalayan cuisine is influenced by the geography and climate of the region. It includes dishes that are hearty and warming, often featuring ingredients like barley, buckwheat, and yak meat. Popular dishes include momos, thukpa, and tingmo.
